# Idea Generation Skill

Use this skill when the user asks for new marketing ideas, viral publicity campaigns, or developer growth strategies to drive adoption and awareness of the repository.

## Guidelines

- Focus on business-oriented publicity, viral developer hooks, marketing campaigns, and growth experiments.
- Output ONLY the brilliant publicity and marketing ideas, formatted with clean headers and bullet points.
- Provide high-energy, creative, and out-of-the-box marketing thinking.
- Structure each idea with:
  - **Campaign / Publicity Idea**: The core marketing, growth, or publicity concept.
  - **Why it drives adoption**: The business impact, repo growth, and developer acquisition value.
- Do not include meta-commentary, preambles, or formatting rules in the output.
